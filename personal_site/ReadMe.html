> SUBJECT: INTRODUCTION

Welcome to my retro themed digital home. I'm a creator, world-builder, and lover of retro things. I spend most of time either working on a creative work or crafting lore for the world of Illorim and a little bit documenting my favorite corners of human media.

> WHY DOES IT LOOK LIKE THIS?

In the modern web, everything is smooth, rounded, and sanitized. I built this site as a 1995 time capsule because that era represented a "Wild West" of personal expression. It was a simpler time. Here, the user is in control of the desktop. It's a reminder of a time when the internet felt like a place you went to, rather than a place you were always trapped in.

> HOW TO USE THIS TERMINAL

- The Desktop: Use the icons to navigate to different sectors (Illorim, Lists, etc).
--- .txt: Simple subpages.
--- .exe: Subpages with a bit more to them.
--- .LNK: Redirect you to external sites.
- The Start Bar: If you get lost, the Start menu in the bottom left acts as your master of navigation.
- X Button: Closing a window will return you to the main desktop.
- The Explorer: The file explorer containes various related writing to Illorim.
